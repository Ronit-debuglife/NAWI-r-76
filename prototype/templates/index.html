Repeatability Test (clause A.4.10)

Requirement: Δ = (I_max - I_min) ≤ |MPE|

Per OIML R-76 clause A.4.10, two series of weighings shall be performed, one at approximately 0.5 Max and one at approximately Max (minimum 3 weighings per load level). The spread between maximum and minimum indication must not exceed the absolute MPE for that load.
